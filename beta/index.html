What you're signing up for.

  • Early access

    Try Aubergenie on your iPhone before it launches. It's free, through TestFlight, Apple's free app for trying apps before they launch. Pricing comes later, and everyone on the list hears before launch.

  • A few rough edges

    Things will wobble now and then. Spotting them is half the job. Your recipes live in your own iCloud, so nothing you add is lost when the beta ends.

  • Your say

    Tell me what you love and what drives you mad, and I'll use it to decide what gets built next.

  • A few at a time

    I'm starting small and letting more people in as it improves. If you're not in the first group, your email stays on the list.

From sign-up to first dinner.

  1. Leave your email with the box ticked.

    That's all for now.

  2. When there's a space, I'll email you.

    A short note from me first. Then Apple emails your TestFlight invite (I give Apple your email address for that).

  3. TestFlight installs the app.

    TestFlight is Apple's free app for trying apps early. Tap the link in Apple's email and Aubergenie lands on your Home Screen like any other app.

  4. Cook something, then tell me how it went.

    Whenever suits. One line is plenty, and if life gets busy, that's useful to know too.

Anyone who cooks is welcome: keen or reluctant, weeknights only or long weekend projects. You'll need an iPhone 11, SE (2nd generation) or newer, on iOS 26.4 or later (Settings › General › About). Bringing your own recipes with the AI switched off is just as useful.
