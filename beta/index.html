Test Aubergenie early · iPhone

Help me test Aubergenie.

It's a little iPhone app that makes cooking fun again. Before it launches, I'd love a small group of people to try it and tell me honestly what works and what doesn't. Keen cook, or never really cooked? You're exactly who I need.

Free, with no ads and no tracking. I'll email when there's a space, and the day it launches. No spam, and you can leave in one step. Privacy

From sign-up to first dinner.

  1. Leave your email with the box ticked.

    I'm letting people in a few at a time. If you're not in the first group, you stay on the list.

  2. When there's a space, I'll email you.

    A short note from me first. Then Apple emails your TestFlight invite (I give Apple your email address for that).

  3. TestFlight installs the app.

    TestFlight is Apple's free app for trying apps early. Tap the link in Apple's email and Aubergenie lands on your Home Screen like any other app.

  4. Cook something, then tell me how it went.

    Whenever suits. One line is plenty, and if life gets busy, that's useful to know too.

It's free, and it will have a few rough edges: telling me about them is the most useful thing you can do. Your recipes live in your own iCloud, so nothing you add is lost when testing ends. You'll need an iPhone 11, SE (2nd generation) or newer, on iOS 26.4 or later (Settings › General › About).

Hi, I'm Nathan.

I wanted all my recipes in one place: easy to find, easy to cook, and easy to share with family and friends. So I built Aubergenie. Now it helps me find something to cook, and then make it. I'd love you to try it early and tell me what would make it better.
