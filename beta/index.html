The Aubergenie beta · iPhone

Help me test Aubergenie.

It's a little iPhone app that makes cooking fun again: ask for any dish, keep the recipes you love, and cook them one step at a time. Before it launches, I'd love a small group of home cooks to try it and tell me honestly what works and what doesn't.

What you're signing up for.

  • Early access

    Try Aubergenie on your iPhone before it launches. It's free, through Apple's TestFlight.

  • A few rough edges

    Things will wobble now and then. Spotting them is half the job.

  • Your say

    Tell me what you love and what drives you mad, and I'll use it to decide what gets built next.

  • A small first group

From sign-up to first dinner.

  1. Leave your email with the box ticked.

    That's all for now.

  2. When there's a space, I'll email you.

    It comes from me, with a link to join.

  3. Apple's TestFlight installs the app.

    Tap the link in Apple's email and Aubergenie lands on your Home Screen like any other app.

  4. Cook something, then tell me how it went.

    A quick message is plenty. Screenshots of anything odd are gold.

Anyone who cooks is welcome: keen or reluctant, weeknights only or long weekend projects. You'll need an iPhone on iOS 26.4 or later. Bringing your own recipes with the AI switched off is just as useful.
