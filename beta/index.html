The Aubergenie iPhone beta

Help me test Aubergenie.

I'm looking for a small group of home cooks to try an early version on iPhone, and to tell me honestly what works and what doesn't.

Three quick questions (optional, to help me pick a varied first group)

How often do you cook?

Where do most of your recipes live now?

Please leave out health or allergy details. The beta needs an iPhone on iOS 26.4 or later.

Testing places are limited; I'll contact you if one becomes available. You'll also hear when Aubergenie launches. Untick the box if launch news is all you'd like. Privacy

What to expect.

Aubergenie is early, honest and a little unfinished. That's exactly why your view matters.

  • Early access

    Try Aubergenie on your iPhone before anyone else, free, through Apple's TestFlight.

  • A few rough edges

    Things will occasionally wobble or change between versions. That's what testing is for.

  • Your say

    Tell me what delights you and what gets in the way. Your feedback shapes what Aubergenie becomes.

  • A small first group

    I'm starting with around a dozen testers. Places are limited; I'll contact you if one becomes available.

How it works.

  1. Leave your email, with the box ticked.

    That puts you on the testers' list as well as the launch list.

  2. When a place opens, I'll email you.

    A personal note from me, not an automatic invitation.

  3. Apple's TestFlight installs the app.

    Tap the link in Apple's email and Aubergenie appears on your Home Screen like any other app.

  4. Cook something, then tell me how it went.

    A quick message is plenty. Screenshots of anything odd are gold.

Anyone who cooks is welcome: keen or reluctant, weeknights only or long weekend projects. You'll need an iPhone on iOS 26.4 or later. Bringing your own recipes with the AI switched off is just as useful.

Questions first? Write to hello@aubergenieapp.com. I read every message.Nathan, who makes Aubergenie
