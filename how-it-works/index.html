Sunday night. Seven dinners to think of, one trip to the shop.

From plan to plate.

  1. Plan the week.

    Plan My Week suggests from your own recipes and says why. Cook once, eat twice: it slots leftovers into another meal and warns when they've been in the fridge more than three days. Set a calorie or protein goal if you like, and it keeps count.

    The rings show what's planned against your goal, and what you've eaten so far. Goals are optional: leave them off and it simply plans your meals.

  2. Shop one list.

    Merged, in aisle order. With iCloud on, it follows you between your devices.

Good to know.

  • Free while I test it. I haven't set a price yet: you'll hear it from me before launch, and you'll never be charged unless you choose to be.
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac. Everything syncs through your own iCloud. Not on Android, sorry.
  • The AI is optional. It only helps when you ask, it has a monthly allowance while I test it, and anything it wrote says so. Saving, planning, shopping and cooking work without it, and without an account.
  • Your recipes are yours. Export them all, any time, as open recipe files with their photos.
  • No ads, no tracking. Your recipes only reach my server when you ask the AI for help. Nothing is ever sold.
