Satay Chicken

35 min · Serves 4 · Thai

Somewhere between the weekly shop and the same five dinners, cooking stopped being fun.

Aubergenie brings the spark back.

It takes care of the thinking, the lists and the timings, so you can enjoy the chopping, the sizzle and the first bite.

  1. Make a wish.

    Type a craving, a mood or whatever's left in the fridge. Aubergenie writes a recipe around your tastes, your allergies and your kitchen.

  2. Keep what you love.

    Paste a link, snap Nan's handwritten card or a cookbook page, or save one a friend sends. Each lands as one clean page, ready to cook.

  3. Cook with ease.

    Plan the week, shop one list in aisle order, then cook step by step in big type, with timers that keep count for you.

A glimpse of the app

Tonight's still open.

That's how Aubergenie greets you. No feed to scroll, no adverts, no life story before the ingredients. Just one question: what would you like to make?

Type it, photograph it or paste a link, and a recipe appears, ready to cook. Stuck for ideas? A couple are waiting to get you started.

The AI only runs when you ask, and you can switch it off in Settings. Bringing your own recipes works without it. How it works
