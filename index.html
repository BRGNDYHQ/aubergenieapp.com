Swipe for more

You bring the appetite. It does the rest.

  • Ask for anything.

    "Something cosy with the leftover chicken." "A quick veggie dinner for four." Aubergenie writes a proper recipe around what you like, what you can't eat and the kit you've actually got.

  • Keep the good ones.

    Paste a link, snap Nan's handwritten card or a cookbook page, or save one a friend sends. Each one becomes a clean, easy-to-follow recipe.

  • Cook without the faff.

    Plan the week, shop one list sorted by aisle, then cook one step at a time in big type. Timers ring even with your phone locked.
